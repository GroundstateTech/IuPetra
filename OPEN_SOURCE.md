# IuPetra Open-Source Philosophy

IuPetra is licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later**.

The project is intended to be inspected, challenged, reproduced, modified, and improved by the community. The GPL is intentional: anyone may use, study, modify, and redistribute the covered source, but distributed covered derivatives must preserve the GPL's source-code freedoms.

Open-sourcing IuPetra does not surrender copyright. Copyright remains with the applicable copyright holders, including contributors for their contributions unless separately agreed. Groundstate/IuPetra names, logos, artwork, and official-project identity are separate from the source-code license, and forks must not imply official Groundstate endorsement without permission.

Scientific criticism and replication are particularly welcome. Pattern-detection heuristics must remain clearly distinguished from authoritative NASA/JPL hazard or impact-risk determinations.

See `LICENSE` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
