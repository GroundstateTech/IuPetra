# Standalone operation

IuPetra runs as a local research and evidence-review workspace. Previously downloaded datasets, normalization, analysis, provenance generation, reports, and the local dashboard require no Groundstate account or identity service.

NASA/JPL and other public endpoints are optional data sources, not authentication authorities. Network failure may make data stale, but it must not erase local evidence or prevent review of existing reports.

Accounts and organization-wide identity are intentionally outside the current scope. Any future collaboration connector must remain optional and must preserve local provenance, exportability, and reproducibility.
